# 2-Minute Demo Script: Ballistic Intelligence Platform
## Narrative Version - No Numbers Focus

**Duration:** 2 minutes  
**Audience:** 5 people  
**Style:** Story-driven, value-focused, conversational

---

## Opening (0:00 - 0:20)

**[Screen: Dashboard Landing Page]**

**Speaker:**
"Imagine you're trying to find the next breakthrough cybersecurity company before everyone else does. You're drowning in fragmented data - Crunchbase shows funding, LinkedIn shows teams, news articles show sentiment, but nothing connects the dots."

**[Pause for effect]**

"That's the problem we solved. Welcome to Ballistic Intelligence Platform - where scattered data becomes strategic insight."

---

## Section 1: The Market Landscape (0:20 - 0:50)

**[Screen: Trending Sectors Tab]**

**Speaker:**
"Let's start with the big picture. The cybersecurity market isn't just growing - it's exploding in different directions."

**[Action: Gesture across the sector cards]**

"Cloud Security, Identity Management, Data Protection, Network Security - each sector tells a different story. But which one has real momentum right now?"

**[Action: Point to Cloud Security at the top]**

"Our platform doesn't just show you who raised money. It shows you where the market is actually moving. Cloud Security is leading because we're tracking funding velocity, market activity, and real-time sentiment - all combined into a single momentum score."

**[Action: Click on Cloud Security to open details]**

"Look at this - the competitive landscape, investment trends, emerging technologies. This is the sector intelligence that helps you decide where to focus your attention."

**[Action: Close and gesture to other sectors]**

"Every sector ranked by actual momentum, not just who has the biggest war chest. Because sometimes the fastest-growing sector isn't the most funded one."

## Section 2: Company Intelligence (0:50 - 1:25)

**[Screen: Switch to Market Intelligence tab]**

**Speaker:**
"Now let's go deeper. Here's where most platforms stop - they show you a list of companies with basic funding data. We're just getting started."

"This is live intelligence, pulled in real-time from multiple sources. Not static data from last quarter."

"AI-powered sentiment analysis tells you what the market thinks about this company right now. Recent mentions show you if they're gaining mindshare. Patent filings reveal if they're building defensible technology."

"We automatically identify their competitive landscape - who they're really competing against, not just who's in the same category."

**[Action: Point to growth indicators]**

"Verified leadership teams - real names, real titles, real people. Because you're not investing in a pitch deck, you're investing in people."

## Presenter Body Language & Delivery

### Vocal Variety:
- **Slow down** for key value propositions
- **Speed up** when showing features
- **Pause** after important reveals
- **Emphasize** words like "real-time," "momentum," "intelligence"

### Gestures:
- **Open palms** when explaining problems (vulnerability)
- **Point** to specific screen elements
- **Sweep** across the screen for "big picture" moments
- **Tap** the screen for emphasis on key data points

### Eye Contact:
- **Look at audience** when asking questions
- **Look at screen** when demonstrating features
- **Return to audience** for key insights
- **Scan all 5 people** throughout presentation

### Energy Levels:
- **Start calm** - establish credibility
- **Build energy** - as you show features
- **Peak energy** - at growth indicators and insights
- **Confident close** - ready for questions

## Handling Different Audience Reactions

### If They're Engaged (Nodding, Leaning Forward):
- Maintain pace
- Add more detail on features they react to
- Use phrases like "You see how this works..."
- Invite questions with eye contact

### If They're Skeptical (Arms Crossed, Frowning):
- Emphasize data sources and accuracy
- Show specific examples
- Use phrases like "Let me show you exactly how..."
- Address concerns directly: "I know what you're thinking..."

### If They're Distracted (Looking at Phones, Talking):
- Use a question to re-engage: "Imagine you're trying to..."
- Increase energy and vocal variety
- Show the most impressive feature immediately
- Make eye contact with engaged person

### If They're Confused (Puzzled Looks):
- Slow down
- Use simpler language
- Add more context: "Think of it like..."
- Ask: "Does that make sense?" before moving on

## Success Indicators During Demo

### Positive Signs:
- ✅ Leaning forward
- ✅ Asking specific questions
- ✅ Taking notes
- ✅ Pointing at screen
- ✅ Discussing among themselves
- ✅ Asking about pricing/access
- ✅ Mentioning specific use cases

### Neutral Signs:
- ⚠️ Polite but passive
- ⚠️ Waiting for you to finish
- ⚠️ Generic questions
- ⚠️ Checking time

### Negative Signs:
- ❌ Looking at phones
- ❌ Side conversations
- ❌ Checking watches
- ❌ Asking to move faster
- ❌ Comparing to competitors negatively

### Recovery Strategies:
If you see negative signs:
1. **Ask a question** to re-engage
2. **Show the most impressive feature** immediately
3. **Relate to their specific pain point**
4. **Offer to customize** the demo to their needs
5. **Acknowledge time** and offer to schedule follow-up
