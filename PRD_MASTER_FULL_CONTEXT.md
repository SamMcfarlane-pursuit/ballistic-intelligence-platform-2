## 1. Problem Statement

### The Core Problem

Investment analysts and corporate development teams dedicate between 5 to 10 hours per week manually gathering, verifying, and synthesizing cybersecurity market intelligence from highly fragmented, disparate data sources. The current process requires switching between costly platforms like PitchBook, Crunchbase, and LinkedIn, resulting in slow, costly, and often outdated insights.

### Detailed Pain Points

| Pain Point | Description | Impact |
|------------|-------------|--------|
| **Excessive Time Waste** | Analysts spend 30-50% of their workday on data aggregation. It typically takes 3 hours to prepare a single company's profile for an Investment Committee (IC) meeting. | 5-10 hours per week lost to manual aggregation, significantly increasing cost-per-deal analysis. |
| **Lagging Insights** | Many databases rely on quarterly or monthly updates, causing investment teams to miss critical, real-time market movements, such as unexpected financing rounds. | Competitors who use proprietary sourcing can identify and act on opportunities 2-3 months earlier. |
| **High Cost, Low Utilization** | Organizations purchase licenses (often $10K-$40K/seat) for multiple tools, but 60% of seats are utilized fewer than 10 times per month. | Significant budget waste; 40% of the annual spend covers occasional users who need only quick summaries. |
| **Incomplete & Inconsistent Data** | There is no single source that synthesizes funding, real-time sentiment, patent strength, and competitive context. Data consistency is low, with competitor tools often exhibiting 10-20% null values. | Decisions are based on partial or inconsistent data, increasing investment risk. |

## 3. Users & Needs

### Primary Users

| User Persona | Role & Usage | Success Metric (Key Performance Indicator) |
|--------------|--------------|---------------------------------------------|
| **Investment Analyst/Associate (Power User)** | Daily due diligence, initial deal sourcing, presentation preparation. | Reducing IC preparation time to under 30 minutes. |

### Key User Needs (Detailed)

1. **Unified Intelligence Hub:** Users need a single, reliable source for comprehensive company intelligence across all dimensions (funding, team, patents, news).

2. **Real-Time Market Intelligence:** Users must see funding announcements and market shifts as they happen to maintain a competitive edge.

3. **Momentum Scoring:** A quantifiable, proprietary score (0-100) is required to instantly assess a company's market traction, sentiment, and hiring velocity.

4. **Fast IC Preparation:** The platform must reduce the administrative time required for synthesizing data for Investment Committee presentations.

5. **One-Click Export:** Users require instant export of filtered and complete data to CSV for custom financial modeling and analysis, without manual cleanup.

6. **Competitive Landscape:** Users need to quickly map and track the competitive threats and opportunities for their portfolio companies.

7. **Sector Trends:** A high-level view of which of the 7 sectors are heating up or cooling down, driven by data.

8. **Verified Leadership:** A critical need to verify the experience and stability of key leadership figures (CEO, CTO, CISO).

9. **Patent Intelligence:** The ability to instantly assess the strength of a target company's IP portfolio by linking to USPTO data.

10. **Data Trust:** The highest priority is absolute trust in the data; analysts will not use the tool if they find more than 1-2 errors per month.

11. **Easy Access:** The platform must be intuitive, requiring zero formal training to use.

12. **Unlimited Team Access:** The pricing model must support high-volume team use without punitive per-seat charges.

### Feature 4: Advanced Filtering System (Full Requirements)

**Goal:** Allow users to rapidly narrow the 233-company universe to a prioritized shortlist.

| Requirement | Priority | Details |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| **Filter by Sector** | [P0] | Checkboxes for all 7 defined sectors (e.g., Cloud Security, XDR, Identity). Must support multi-select. Grid must update instantly upon selection/deselection. |
| **Filter by Region** | [P0] | Radio buttons or checkboxes for key geographic regions: North America, Western Europe, Middle East, Asia Pacific. |
| **Filter by Funding Stage** | [P0] | Multi-select checkboxes for standard venture stages: Seed, Series A, Series B, Series C, Series D+. |

| **Clear All Filters** | [P0] | A highly visible button to reset all active filters, returning the display to the full 233-company dataset. |
| **Search by Company Name** | [P1] | An always-visible search bar with instant, fuzzy matching autocomplete for quick lookup. |

### Feature 8: Data Quality & Validation

**System Goal:** 100% Data Trust, Zero User-Facing Errors

| Requirement | Priority | Details |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| **5-Stage Background Validation Pipeline** | [P0] | A fully automated, background process must validate all data on ingestion: 1) Source Security Scan, 2) Data Quality Check (type, format), 3) Cross-Source Verification (e.g., Crunchbase vs. Pitchbook), 4) Business Logic check (e.g., Valuation > Funding), 5) Final Sanitization. This process must be invisible and non-blocking to the user. |
| **Zero Null Values Mandate** | [P0] | The application code must implement logic to prevent the display of null, undefined, or empty strings in the UI. Unavailable data must be translated and displayed as "N/A" or "Data not yet available" (using a subtle, neutral color). |
| **Internal Issue Logging** | [P0] | A mechanism must be in place to log all validation errors and data ingestion failures to an internal analytics tool. The user interface must never surface a data error. |
| **Verified Leadership Flag** | [P1] | Leadership data (CEO, CTO) must include a "Verified" badge only after cross-referencing with LinkedIn profiles and official company websites. |
