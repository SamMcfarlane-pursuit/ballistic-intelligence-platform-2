## 1. Typical Data Query Frequency

### Industry Standard Usage Patterns

#### **Daily Users (Power Users)**
**Who:** Investment analysts, associates, researchers  
**Frequency:** 5-20 queries per day  
**Use Cases:**
- Morning market scan for new funding announcements
- Due diligence research on target companies
- Competitive landscape analysis
- Portfolio company monitoring
- Deal sourcing and pipeline management

**Typical Workflow:**
```
8:00 AM  - Check overnight funding announcements
9:00 AM  - Research 3-5 companies for partner meeting
11:00 AM - Deep dive on due diligence target
2:00 PM  - Competitive analysis for portfolio company
4:00 PM  - Update deal pipeline with new prospects
```

**Pain Points:**
- ❌ Switching between multiple platforms (PitchBook, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, news sites)
- ❌ Manual data aggregation and Excel exports
- ❌ Stale data requiring constant refreshes
- ❌ No unified view of company intelligence

---

#### **Weekly Users (Regular Users)**
**Who:** Partners, VPs, senior analysts  
**Frequency:** 10-30 queries per week  
**Use Cases:**
- Weekly portfolio reviews
- Investment committee preparation
- Market trend analysis
- Sector deep dives
- Board meeting preparation

**Pain Points:**
- ❌ Time-consuming data gathering (2-3 hours per IC)
- ❌ Inconsistent data formats across team members
- ❌ Difficulty tracking changes since last review
- ❌ Manual creation of comparison charts


#### **Monthly/Ad-Hoc Users (Occasional Users)**
**Who:** Executives, board members, limited partners  
**Frequency:** 5-15 queries per month  
**Use Cases:**
- Quarterly board meetings
- LP reporting
- Strategic planning sessions
- Major deal evaluations

**Pain Points:**
- ❌ Paying for full licenses with minimal usage
- ❌ Steep learning curve for infrequent users
- ❌ Difficulty finding specific data quickly

### Usage Reality Check

**The 80/20 Problem:**
- 📊 **80% of queries** come from **20% of users** (power users)
- 📊 **60% of licenses** are underutilized (< 10 queries/month)
- 📊 **40% of annual spend** goes to occasional users who could use shared access

**Example: Mid-Size Firm**
```
Total Spend: $500K/year
Active Users: 25 people

Breakdown:
- 5 power users (20%) → 80% of queries → $100K value
- 10 regular users (40%) → 15% of queries → $200K value
- 10 occasional users (40%) → 5% of queries → $200K value (WASTE)

Actual Value Delivered: ~$300K
Wasted Spend: ~$200K (40%)
```

**Time Costs:**

- Manual data entry: 2-5 hours/week per team = **$20K-50K/year**

**Opportunity Costs:**
- Missed deals due to slow research
- Poor decisions from incomplete data
- Delayed IC meetings waiting for data
- Lost competitive advantage

**Total True Cost = Subscription + Time + Opportunity**
- Small Firm: $150K-400K/year
- Mid-Size Firm: $600K-1.5M/year
- Large Firm: $2M-5M+/year
