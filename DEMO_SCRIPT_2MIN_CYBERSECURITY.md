# 2-Minute Demo Script: Ballistic Intelligence Platform
## Cybersecurity Startup Presentation for 5-Person Audience

**Duration:** 2 minutes  
**Audience:** 5 people (investors, executives, or potential clients)  
**Objective:** Demonstrate comprehensive intelligence capabilities for cybersecurity market analysis

---

## Script Structure

### Opening (0:00 - 0:15) - 15 seconds

**[Screen: Dashboard Landing Page]**

**Speaker:**
"Welcome to Ballistic Intelligence Platform - your command center for cybersecurity market intelligence. In the next two minutes, I'll show you how we transform fragmented data from multiple sources into actionable insights that drive investment decisions."

**[Action: Gesture to screen showing the full dashboard]**

---

### Section 1: Trending Sectors Overview (0:15 - 0:45) - 30 seconds

**[Screen: Trending Sectors Tab - Show 7 sectors with momentum scores]**

**Speaker:**
"First, our Trending Sectors view. We analyze 233 cybersecurity companies across 7 key sectors in real-time."

**[Action: Point to Cloud Security sector - #1 ranked]**

"Cloud Security leads with a momentum score of 94 - that's 47 companies, $8.2 billion in total funding, and 12% growth. Our algorithm combines funding velocity, market activity, and AI-powered sentiment analysis."

**[Action: Click on Cloud Security card to show sector details]**

"Each sector shows investment trends, key players, and emerging technologies. Notice the competitive landscape - we're tracking Zscaler, Okta, and 45 other innovators."

**[Action: Close sector details, scroll to show other sectors]**

"Identity Management, Data Protection, Network Security - all ranked by real momentum, not just funding size."

---

### Section 2: Market Intelligence Grid (0:45 - 1:15) - 30 seconds

**[Screen: Switch to Market Intelligence tab]**

**Speaker:**
"Now, the Market Intelligence grid - our 3-column layout displays 6 companies per page with verified data."

**[Action: Point to first company card - e.g., Wiz]**

"Each card shows: company name, sector, location, founding year, total funding, last round details, and lead investor. But here's what sets us apart..."

**[Action: Click on a company card to open Enhanced Company Dialog]**

**[Screen: Enhanced Company Dialog opens]**

"Real-time intelligence from BrightData and Crunchbase APIs. Look at this:"

**[Action: Point to Intelligence Insights section]**

- "AI-powered sentiment analysis - Positive news sentiment
- Recent mentions - 47 in the last 30 days
- Patent portfolio - 12 filed patents
- Market position - Growing
- Competitive landscape - we identify top 5 competitors automatically"

**[Action: Scroll to Leadership Team section]**

"Verified leadership teams with actual names and titles - CEO, CTO, VP of Engineering. No placeholder data."

**[Action: Point to growth indicators]**

"Growth indicators show 35% hiring velocity, 42% funding momentum, and 28% news volume - all calculated from real data sources."

---

### Section 3: Patent Deep Dive (1:15 - 1:35) - 20 seconds

**[Screen: Close dialog, switch to Patent Deep Dive tab]**

**Speaker:**
"Patent intelligence - we track 100+ patents across the cybersecurity landscape."

**[Action: Point to patent cards showing innovation scores]**

"Each patent is scored for novelty and innovation potential. This Zero-Trust Architecture patent from Okta has a 94 novelty score and high innovation potential."

**[Action: Scroll through patents]**

"Filter by sector, company, or innovation level. See which companies are building defensible IP moats."

---

### Section 4: Data Export & Filters (1:35 - 1:55) - 20 seconds

**[Screen: Return to Market Intelligence, show filter panel]**

**Speaker:**
"Powerful filtering - by sector, region, funding stage, investor, and time period."

**[Action: Apply filter - e.g., "Cloud Security" + "North America" + "Series B"]**

"Watch the grid update instantly. Now we're seeing only Series B Cloud Security companies in North America."

**[Action: Point to Export button (if visible) or mention it]**

"Export any view to CSV with one click - complete with intelligence metrics, leadership data, and growth indicators. Perfect for board presentations or due diligence."

**[Action: Show display mode toggle - Grid to List view]**

"Switch between grid and list views depending on your analysis needs."

---

### Closing (1:55 - 2:00) - 5 seconds

**[Screen: Return to full dashboard view]**

**Speaker:**
"Ballistic Intelligence Platform - real-time data, AI-powered insights, and verified intelligence for cybersecurity market leaders. Questions?"

**[Action: Pause on dashboard, open for questions]**

## Presenter Notes

### Before Demo:
- [ ] Open platform in browser, full screen mode
- [ ] Clear browser cache for fresh data load
- [ ] Have 2-3 specific companies pre-selected to showcase (e.g., Wiz, CrowdStrike, Okta)
- [ ] Test all transitions and clicks
- [ ] Ensure internet connection is stable for API calls
- [ ] Have backup screenshots in case of technical issues

### During Demo:
- **Pace:** Speak clearly but quickly - you have 2 minutes
- **Gestures:** Point to specific elements on screen as you mention them
- **Eye Contact:** Look at audience between screen references
- **Confidence:** Know your numbers - 233 companies, 7 sectors, etc.
- **Smooth Transitions:** Practice clicking between tabs smoothly

### Timing Checkpoints:
- 0:15 - Should be on Trending Sectors
- 0:45 - Should be on Market Intelligence
- 1:15 - Should be on Patent Deep Dive
- 1:35 - Should be showing filters
- 2:00 - Should be finished, ready for questions

### Common Questions to Prepare For:
1. **"What's your data source?"**
   - "We integrate BrightData and Crunchbase APIs in real-time, plus curated Google Sheets data, all validated through our proprietary data quality system."

2. **"How often is data updated?"**
   - "Real-time API integration with 5-minute intelligent caching for performance. Fresh data on every company view."

3. **"Can we export this data?"**
   - "Yes, one-click CSV export with customizable columns including all intelligence metrics, leadership data, and growth indicators."

4. **"How accurate is the sentiment analysis?"**
   - "Our AI analyzes news mentions, SEC filings, and patent activity. We show confidence levels and source attribution for transparency."

5. **"What makes this different from Crunchbase or PitchBook?"**
   - "We combine multiple data sources, add AI-powered intelligence layers, focus specifically on cybersecurity, and provide momentum scoring that predicts market movement, not just historical funding data."

## Script Variations by Audience Size

### For 1-2 people (more interactive):
- Pause after each section for questions
- Ask what sectors they're most interested in
- Customize filters to their preferences in real-time
- Spend more time on specific companies they mention

### For 5-10 people (presentation mode):
- Stick to script timing strictly
- Save questions for end
- Use broader examples that appeal to diverse interests
- Emphasize scalability and team collaboration features

### For 20+ people (conference/webinar):
- Add more context about market size and opportunity
- Include customer testimonials or case studies
- Show ROI calculations
- Emphasize competitive advantages more strongly
